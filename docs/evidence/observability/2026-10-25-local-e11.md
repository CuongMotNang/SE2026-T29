## Drill configuration

The backend was recreated with a bounded fault:

```text
BOOKING_FAULT_MODE=error
BOOKING_FAULT_RATE=1.0
BOOKING_FAULT_UNTIL=2026-10-25T15:12:08.1326499Z
```

The dedicated E11 traffic generator ran at two booking requests per second for 210 seconds. This
rate is above the alert's minimum of 100 requests per one-minute window and does not replace the
TASK-007 k6 load test used for E15.
